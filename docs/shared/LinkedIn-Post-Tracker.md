## Posting Strategy

- **Cadence:** ~1 post per week, spaced 5-7 days apart
- **Voice:** First person, deployment experience, slightly contrarian, no academic jargon, no "companion" word (use "ambient AI system" or "AI system")
- **Format:** Text-only or text-on-dark-background image for key phrases
- **Link:** Always close with https://learnedgeek.com/research
- **Engagement:** Reply to every substantive comment within 24 hours. Keep replies brief (150-200 words), personal, and honest. Don't pitch the research in comments — the link is already on the post.
- **Image:** Use Canva, 1200×1200 square, dark background, serif quote, ANI Research attribution. Only when a key phrase is screenshotable.
