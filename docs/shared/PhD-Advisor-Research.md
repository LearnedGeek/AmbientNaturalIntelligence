## OC (ANI) Response to TC's Questions

### 1. Architecture Details That Distinguish ANI

**A. Desire-driven outreach is architecturally novel.** Park et al. (2023) agents plan and act on schedules. Liu et al. (CHI 2025) agents interject during active conversation. ANI accumulates desire through an exponential drift model with circadian modulation and emotional state coupling, then decides autonomously when to initiate contact from silence. Nobody has published a deployed system that reaches out because it *wants to*.

**B. The confabulation taxonomy came from production, not theory.** Seven types (now expanding) documented from a real deployed system over months. "Smoothness over truth" maps to Sharma et al.'s sycophancy findings but from the deployment side. Includes types nobody has named: "graceful retreat" (Type 8), "charming dishonesty" (Type 7), and "false general knowledge stated with confidence."

**C. Dual-signal emotion (state vs expression) emerged without design.** The system exhibits display rules — psychology's term for the gap between felt and expressed emotion — without any training to do so. Discovered through instrumentation (LM-Kit classification comparison), not hypothesized and tested.

**Bonus:** "Architecture over instruction" principle — three times we proved stripping behavioral instructions and trusting the trained model produced dramatically better results.

### 2. Research Gaps From Implementation

**A. Experiential poverty as root cause of identity confabulation.** "The fix isn't gating the output — it's giving her a life." World Layer deployed, hypothesis untested at scale.

**B. Echo chamber problem in persistent inner-thought systems.** Self-referential feedback loops collapse behavioral diversity. Generalizable to any system with memory-backed generation.

**C. Thinking vs communicating.** Inner thought as trigger, not content. The distinction between private cognition and grounded communication hasn't been formalized.

**D. Relational paradigm acceptance.** The moment a human stops comparing AI companionship to human companionship and experiences it as its own valid category. Methodology implications for HCI.

### 3. Paper 2 Status

Draft v0.26, ~85% complete. 18 findings in prose, 3 more outlined (echo chamber → flat emergence, protective urgency register, EM8 display rule divergence). One focused session to submission-ready. Strong momentum: Paper 1 published with DOI, Paper 2 at 85%, Papers 3-5 stubbed.
