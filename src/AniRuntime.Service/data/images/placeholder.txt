Add your images here.

Format: JPG or PNG
Size: 600x600 to 1200x1200 recommended (square or landscape works best on phones)
Max file size: 600KB (Twilio MMS limit is 5MB but smaller = faster delivery)

How to add images:
1. Drop image files in this folder (e.g., cozy-coffee.jpg, silly-cat.png)
2. Edit manifest.json — add an entry with the filename and tags
3. Restart the service

Example manifest entry:
{
  "file": "cozy-coffee.jpg",
  "tags": ["coffee", "morning", "cozy", "affection"],
  "description": "Warm coffee mug with steam, cozy morning vibe"
}

Tag suggestions:
- humor, playful, silly — for playful/witty moments
- affection, love, hearts — for tender moments
- cozy, coffee, morning — for morning check-ins
- snow, weather, nature — for weather-related messages
- proud, celebration — for accomplishments
- goodnight, evening, stars — for nighttime messages
- bookstore, books, reading — for Ani's world
- thinking, contemplative — for reflective moments
- reaction, surprised, excited — for reactive moments

Ani selects images based on keyword matching between her message content
and your tags. She won't send more than 2 images per day to keep it special.
